\paragraph{Data and protocol.}
All data for the $[[72,12,6]]$ headline experiment are code-capacity samples
with independent $Z$ and $X$ flips on each data qubit at rates
$p_Z=p\,\eta/(\eta+1)$ and $p_X=p/(\eta+1)$, $\eta=20$ (no $Y$ errors are
drawn explicitly; a qubit with both flips carries a $Y$).
The training set is 8{,}000 shots from two sinusoidally drifting streams,
$p(t)=p_0+0.015\sin(2\pi t/500)$ with $p_0\in\{0.02,0.03\}$, so training
$p$ spans $[0.005,0.045]$.
Validation (4{,}000 shots) and test sets ($20{,}000$ shots at $p=0.04$,
inside the training range; $10{,}000$ shots at $p=0.06$, outside it) are drawn
with separate random seeds, so no evaluation shot shares a random stream with
a training shot.
The checkpoint is chosen by the lowest validation failure count over 20
epochs, and each test set is scored once on that checkpoint.
We repeat training with five seeds (initialisation and minibatch order).
The GNN has 3 message-passing layers, hidden width 32 (39{,}329 parameters),
and is trained with AdamW (learning rate $2\times10^{-3}$, weight decay
$10^{-4}$, batch 64, gradient clipping at 1) on the focal loss
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}=-\frac{1}{Bn}\sum_{b,j}\alpha_t\,(1-p_t)^{\gamma}\log p_t,\quad
p_t=\begin{cases}\sigma(-(\ell_j+\delta_j)) & e_{Z,j}=1\\
1-\sigma(-(\ell_j+\delta_j)) & e_{Z,j}=0\end{cases},
\end{equation}
with $\alpha=0.25$, $\gamma=2$, where $\ell_j=\log((1-p)/p)$ is the prior LLR
and $\delta_j$ the GNN correction.
The corrected LLRs $\ell_j+\delta_j$ are passed to 10-iteration flooding
min-sum BP (scaling 0.8).

GNN pre-processing reduces the failure rate of 10-iteration BP by
$12.5\%\pm0.6\%$ at $p=0.04$ and $10.6\%\pm0.7\%$ at $p=0.06$, which lies
outside the training range; every seed is significant
($p<10^{-36}$, McNemar). The gain comes mostly from convergence: shots where
BP fails to reproduce the syndrome fall from 1{,}740 to 838--975 of 20{,}000.
The gain does not close the gap to longer decoding: 100-iteration BP (0.0863)
and BP-OSD (0.0742) both outperform GNN + 10-iteration BP.
